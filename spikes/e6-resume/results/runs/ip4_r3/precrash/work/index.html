PocketSprout Terrarium Kits

PocketSprout is a palm-sized terrarium kit that lets anyone grow a miniature moss garden on a desk or windowsill. Each kit ships with a hand-blown glass capsule, a cork base, live cushion moss, a pinch of volcanic gravel, and a tiny bamboo mister. Assembly takes under five minutes, and the sealed capsule recycles its own moisture, so the garden only needs a light mist once every few weeks. It's a quiet, low-maintenance way to bring a bit of green into small apartments and busy offices.
