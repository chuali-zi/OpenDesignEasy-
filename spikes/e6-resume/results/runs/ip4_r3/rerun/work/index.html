Lanternleaf Tea Co.

Lanternleaf Tea Co. is a tiny fictional tea company that blends hand-picked jasmine leaves with dried yuzu peel and a whisper of vanilla bean. Each tin of our signature "Evening Lantern" blend is filled in small batches of fifty, numbered by hand, and designed to steep a calm, golden cup after long days. We ship in compostable pouches and include a paper bookmark in every order.
