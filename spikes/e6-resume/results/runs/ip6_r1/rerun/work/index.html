Pocket Kettle

The Pocket Kettle is a palm-sized travel kettle that boils a single cup of water in under ninety seconds. Built from food-grade silicone and stainless steel, it folds flat enough to slip into a jacket pocket, plugs into any USB-C charger, and shuts itself off the moment your water reaches a rolling boil. Whether you are camping in the hills or stuck in a hotel room with no kettle in sight, a proper cup of tea is never more than a minute and a half away.
