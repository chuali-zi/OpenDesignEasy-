The Moonbeam Mug

The Moonbeam Mug is a small self-warming travel cup built for chilly morning walks. A coin-sized heater in its base keeps your coffee at a comfortable 60 °C for up to four hours on a single USB-C charge, while the double-walled ceramic shell stays cool enough to hold bare-handed. Each mug is glazed by hand at our two-person workshop in the fictional town of Larkspur Hollow, and a tiny crescent-moon indicator on the lid glows softly when your drink has reached the perfect sipping temperature.
