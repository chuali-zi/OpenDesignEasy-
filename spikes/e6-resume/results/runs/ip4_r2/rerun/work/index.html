Lumen Jar

Lumen Jar is a palm-sized bedside lamp that mimics the glow of a real sunrise. Over the twenty minutes before your alarm, it slowly shifts from deep amber to bright morning gold, gently coaxing you awake without a single jarring beep. Each jar is hand-poured from recycled glass, runs for a week on one charge, and remembers your wake-up schedule even when your phone is off.
