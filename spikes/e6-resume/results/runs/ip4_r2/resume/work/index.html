Moonjar

Moonjar is a small, jar-shaped bedside lamp built for people who hate being startled awake. Instead of a blaring alarm, Moonjar begins glowing thirty minutes before your chosen wake time, slowly brightening from a deep amber to a soft daylight white — mimicking a sunrise on your nightstand. It is handmade from frosted recycled glass, runs a full week on a single USB-C charge, and has exactly one button, because mornings should be simple.
