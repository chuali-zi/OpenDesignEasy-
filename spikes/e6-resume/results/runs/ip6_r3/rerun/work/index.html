The Cloverleaf Lantern

The Cloverleaf Lantern is a pocket-sized camp light with a hand crank on its side: sixty seconds of cranking gives you forty minutes of warm, flicker-free light. It folds flat to the size of a coaster, weighs less than a bar of soap, and its frosted silicone shell doubles as a soft diffuser so it never blinds you in a dark tent. Each lantern ships with a tiny compass in the cap and a lifetime supply of replacement wicks.
