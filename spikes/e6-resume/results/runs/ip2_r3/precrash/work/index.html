PebblePod

PebblePod is a pebble-sized sensor that sits in your houseplant's soil and quietly tracks moisture, light, and temperature. When your fern is thirsty, it sends a gentle nudge to your phone — no buzzing alarms, just a soft reminder and a tip on how much water to give. Each PebblePod runs for a full year on a single coin battery and comes in four matte earth tones.
