Kettle Buddy

Kettle Buddy is a tiny countertop gadget that keeps your tea steeping honest. Twist its dial to pick a leaf — green, oolong, black, or herbal — and it quietly counts down the perfect steep time for that variety. When the gentle chime sounds, your cup is ready: never bitter, never weak. It runs for a full year on a single coin battery, wipes clean with a damp cloth, and fits in the palm of your hand, so it travels from kitchen counter to campsite without complaint.
