The Little Warmer by Ember Mug Co.

The Little Warmer is a palm-sized ceramic disk that keeps any mug of tea or coffee at a gentle 55°C for up to four hours. It runs silently, charges over USB-C in about forty minutes, and shuts itself off the moment you lift your cup. Designed for desks, nightstands, and anyone tired of lukewarm drinks, it ships in three matte colors and weighs less than a deck of cards.
