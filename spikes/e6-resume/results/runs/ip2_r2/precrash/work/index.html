Driftwood Kettle Co.

Our Ember kettle is a hand-cast enamel kettle made in small batches on the Oregon coast. Each one holds just enough water for two generous cups of tea, heats evenly over any stove, and whistles with a soft, low tone we tuned by ear in the workshop. We sand the wooden handle smooth with driftwood oil, which is where our name comes from, and we ship every kettle in a recyclable box with a handwritten card from the person who cast it.
