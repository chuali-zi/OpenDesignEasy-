CopperKettle

CopperKettle is a palm-sized camp kettle that boils half a liter of water in under four minutes on a single alcohol burner tablet. Each one is spun from a single sheet of reclaimed copper, hammered by hand in a two-person workshop in Duluth, Minnesota, and fitted with a birch-wood handle that stays cool even over an open flame. It weighs 210 grams, folds flat enough to slide into a jacket pocket, and carries a lifetime repair promise — if it ever dents or leaks, the makers will hammer it back into shape for free.
