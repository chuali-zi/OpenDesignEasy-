Moss & Ember Tea Lights

Moss & Ember is a two-person workshop in Portland, Maine, that pours small-batch beeswax tea lights blended with cedarwood oil and a pinch of real pine needle ash. Each tin burns for roughly six hours, and for every dozen sold the founders plant one sapling along the Androscoggin River. The candles ship in recycled kraft boxes with a hand-stamped label, and the lids double as tiny reusable coasters.
