Light that grows on sunlight

LumenLeaf is a compact reading lamp that charges itself. Its leaf-shaped solar panel unfolds by day to soak up sunlight, then folds back into a warm, glare-free reading light by night. A single afternoon of sun powers up to twelve hours of soft illumination — no outlets, no batteries to replace, and no cords trailing across your desk.

Designed for readers, campers, and anyone living off the grid, LumenLeaf weighs less than a paperback and fits flat in a backpack. Three brightness levels and an automatic dusk sensor mean it simply works when you need it.

Features

Foldable monocrystalline solar leaf, warm-to-cool adjustable tone, twelve-hour battery life, splash-proof casing, and a recycled aluminum frame — all backed by a five-year warranty.
