The LumenBrew Kettle

Precision heat for the perfect pour-over, every single morning.

The LumenBrew is a smart pour-over kettle designed for people who take their coffee seriously but not their mornings. Its slim gooseneck spout gives you total control over flow rate, while a built-in temperature sensor holds your water within half a degree of your target — from a delicate 80°C for green tea to a rolling 96°C for dark roasts.

Features

  • Variable temperature control from 40°C to 100°C in one-degree steps
  • Hold mode keeps water at temperature for up to 60 minutes
  • Counterbalanced handle and precision gooseneck spout for a steady pour
  • Quiet-boil element that heats a full liter in under four minutes
  • Matte stainless steel body in three finishes: Charcoal, Oat, and Copper

Our Story

LumenBrew began in a small Portland garage in 2021, when two baristas grew tired of kettles that overshot the mark. After dozens of prototypes and one memorable burnt countertop, they landed on a design that pairs café-grade accuracy with a shape that looks at home on any kitchen counter. Every kettle is still assembled and tested by hand before it ships.
