TerraMug: Coffee That Stays Warm, Wherever You Are

The self-heating travel mug built for commuters, campers, and anyone tired of lukewarm mornings.

TerraMug is a 12-ounce insulated travel mug with a slim, battery-powered heating element in its base. Set your preferred drinking temperature between 120°F and 145°F using the small dial on the handle, and TerraMug holds your coffee, tea, or cocoa there for up to six hours on a single charge. It recharges over USB-C, so the same cable that powers your laptop keeps your mug ready for the next day.

Why You'll Love It

The double-walled stainless steel body keeps the outside cool to the touch, and the spill-resistant lid opens with one thumb — handy on a crowded train. TerraMug is fully dishwasher-safe once you twist off the heating base, and it fits in every standard car cup holder we tested, including the awkwardly small ones.

Our Story

TerraMug began in a drafty garage workshop in Portland, where our founder kept abandoning half-finished cups of coffee that had gone cold during long repair projects. After a winter of prototypes — and one memorable melted prototype — the first TerraMug shipped in 2023. Today, every mug is still assembled and tested by our small team before it leaves the workshop.
