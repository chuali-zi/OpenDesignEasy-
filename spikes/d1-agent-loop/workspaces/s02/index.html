Meet LumaBrew, the kettle that cleans itself.

LumaBrew is a countertop smart kettle built for people who love tea but hate scrubbing limescale. Its stainless interior uses a gentle ultrasonic cycle after every tenth boil to dissolve mineral buildup on its own, so the water you taste is always pure — never chalky. Set your preferred temperature between 40°C and 100°C in one-degree steps, and LumaBrew holds it there silently for up to two hours.

Self-cleaning ultrasonic cycle

A 90-second pulse cycle breaks down limescale while you sleep. No vinegar, no brushes, no forgotten descaling tablets.

Degree-perfect temperature control

Dial in exactly 65°C for green tea or 92°C for pour-over coffee. A quiet heating element keeps the water there without re-boiling.

Designed for small kitchens

At just 22 cm tall with a 1-litre capacity, LumaBrew fits under any cabinet and pours with a counterbalanced, drip-free spout.
