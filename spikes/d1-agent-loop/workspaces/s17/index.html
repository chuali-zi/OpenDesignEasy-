Lumen Brew

Café-quality pour-over coffee, without the learning curve.

Lumen Brew is a countertop coffee maker that automates the craft of pour-over brewing. It heats water to the exact temperature your beans need, pulses the pour in slow spirals just like a skilled barista, and finishes a single cup in under four minutes. You choose the roast profile once, and every morning after that tastes like your favorite café.

Why you'll love it

A built-in scale weighs your grounds and adjusts the water ratio automatically, so a strong Monday cup and a light weekend cup are both one tap away. The reusable stainless filter means no paper waste, and the companion app remembers recipes from roasters around the world — or lets you save your own.

Get yours

Lumen Brew ships this fall for $249, with a two-year warranty and a 30-day taste guarantee. If your first bag of beans doesn't taste better than your old machine made it, send it back — no questions asked.
