The lantern that learns your evenings.

Emberlight is a hand-finished ceramic lantern with a quiet brain inside. Over its first week in your home, it notices when you settle in to read, when you cook dinner, and when you wind down for the night — then it begins matching its glow to your rhythm, warming to candlelight as the evening deepens and dimming itself when the house goes still.

Why you'll love it

There are no apps to install and no schedules to program. A single brass dial on the base is the entire interface: turn it to brighten, press it to pause the learning. The warm-white LED core is tuned to 1800K, the color of an actual flame, and the battery lasts three weeks between charges on its woven USB cord.

Our story

Emberlight began in a drafty workshop in Portland, Maine, where our founders kept blowing out candles while tinkering late into the night. They wanted the calm of firelight without the fuss — and built a lantern that pays attention so you don't have to.

Each lantern is glazed, assembled, and tested by hand. Pre-orders open this fall at $129, with free shipping anywhere in the continental US.
