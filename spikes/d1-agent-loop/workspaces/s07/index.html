LumenLeaf

The self-watering smart planter that keeps your plants alive — even when you forget they exist.

LumenLeaf is a countertop planter with a built-in water reservoir, a light-sensing canopy, and a simple companion app. Fill the reservoir once every three weeks, and LumenLeaf meters out exactly the moisture your plant needs based on the soil conditions it measures every hour. No schedules to remember, no wilted basil on the windowsill, no guilt.

Features

A three-liter reservoir quietly feeds the roots from below, so plants drink only what they need. A frosted glass canopy tracks ambient light and gently tilts toward the sunniest spot in the room. When the reservoir runs low, the rim glows a soft amber — and if you ignore that, the app sends one polite notification. Just one.

How It Works

Plant your favorite herb or houseplant in the included mineral substrate, top up the reservoir, and choose the plant type in the app. LumenLeaf's soil sensors handle the rest, adjusting watering to the seasons so your fern thrives in January just as well as it does in July.
