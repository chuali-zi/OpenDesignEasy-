Meet EmberMug Go: Coffee That Never Goes Cold

EmberMug Go is a self-heating travel mug that keeps your drink at exactly the temperature you set — anywhere between 50 °C and 65 °C — for up to six hours on a single charge. A discreet dial on the base lets you pick your perfect heat, and the mug remembers it for next time. Its double-walled stainless steel body stays cool to the touch, fits standard car cup holders, and is fully dishwasher-safe once the battery cap is removed.

Built-in spill-lock lid, USB-C fast charging, and a companion app that gently reminds you when your drink has reached the ideal sipping temperature — because life is too short for lukewarm coffee.
