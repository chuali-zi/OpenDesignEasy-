Sleep Anywhere. Arrive Rested.

DriftWell is a travel pillow built around one simple observation: most travel pillows prop your chin up, which is exactly why your neck hurts after a red-eye. Instead, DriftWell cradles the sides of your head with two firm memory-foam wings, letting your neck stay in a neutral position whether you're upright in seat 34C or leaning against a train window in the Alps.

Why travelers love it

The whole pillow compresses into a pouch the size of a coffee mug, weighs less than a paperback, and has a washable bamboo-blend cover that stays cool against your skin. A discreet buckle clips it to your carry-on so it never becomes one more thing to juggle at the gate.

Made for real journeys

We tested DriftWell on overnight buses in Peru, budget flights across Southeast Asia, and more delayed commuter trains than we care to count. Every prototype was refined by the people actually sleeping on it, and the result is a pillow that earns its place in your bag on every single trip.
