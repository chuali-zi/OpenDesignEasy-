Meet NimbusBrew: Café-Quality Coffee, Zero Guesswork

The smart pour-over coffee maker that remembers exactly how you like your morning cup.

NimbusBrew is a compact countertop brewer that pairs a precision temperature-controlled kettle with a built-in scale and a slow-pour robotic arm. Tell the companion app your bean's roast level and your preferred strength once, and NimbusBrew handles bloom time, water temperature, and pour rhythm automatically. Whether you love a bright Ethiopian light roast or a heavy, chocolatey dark blend, every cup comes out consistent — no scales, timers, or thermometers cluttering your kitchen.

Why We Built It

Our founders spent years chasing the perfect pour-over at home and kept falling short on busy mornings. NimbusBrew was born from a simple idea: great coffee shouldn't require barista training. After two years of prototyping with specialty roasters in Portland, we built a machine that brews like a patient expert — even at 6 a.m.
