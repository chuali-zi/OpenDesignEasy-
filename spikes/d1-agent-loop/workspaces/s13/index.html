Meet LumenBrew: the kettle that knows your coffee.

LumenBrew is a precision pour-over kettle designed for people who treat their morning cup like a ritual, not a routine. Its slim gooseneck spout gives you total control over flow rate, while a quiet heating element brings water to your exact target temperature — anywhere from 40°C to 100°C — and holds it there for up to an hour.

Features

A built-in bloom timer gently pulses when it's time to start your pour, and the counterbalanced handle stays cool and comfortable even during a slow, three-minute brew. The matte steel body resists fingerprints, and the base detaches so the kettle travels freely from counter to table.

Our Story

LumenBrew began in a two-person garage workshop in Portland, where our founders kept ruining good beans with inconsistent water temperature. Three years and forty-one prototypes later, we've built the kettle we always wanted — and we think you'll taste the difference in the very first cup.
