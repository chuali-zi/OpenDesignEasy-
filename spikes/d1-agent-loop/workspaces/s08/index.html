Meet the Lumina Kettle

The Lumina Kettle is a precision pour-over kettle designed for home brewers who care about consistency. Its gooseneck spout gives you slow, controlled pours, while the built-in thermometer on the lid lets you hit your target water temperature without guesswork. The matte-black stainless steel body holds 900 ml, heats quickly on gas, electric, or induction stovetops, and its counterbalanced handle keeps your wrist comfortable through an entire bloom and pour cycle.

We built Lumina after years of juggling separate kettles, clip-on thermometers, and timers. Every detail — from the tight-fitting lid to the easy-read dial — exists to remove one more distraction between you and a great cup of coffee or tea.
