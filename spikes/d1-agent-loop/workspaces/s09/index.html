EmberMug Go: Coffee That Stays Perfect

EmberMug Go is a self-heating travel mug that keeps your coffee at exactly 57°C for up to six hours — no microwave, no thermos roulette. A slim heating element in the base, paired with a quiet temperature sensor, adjusts itself every few seconds so your last sip tastes like your first. It charges over USB-C, fits in any car cup holder, and survives the dishwasher.

Precision Heat

Choose any temperature between 50°C and 65°C with a twist of the base ring. The mug holds it steady within half a degree.

A full charge lasts six hours of heating, and a 20-minute USB-C top-up gets you through the morning commute.

Built for Real Life

Spill-proof lid, one-hand opening, and a ceramic-coated interior that never holds flavors — tea today won't haunt tomorrow's espresso.
