Precision Pour-Over Kettle

The Lumina Kettle: café-quality brewing on your kitchen counter.

Great coffee starts with great water — and the Lumina Kettle gives you complete control over both temperature and flow. Its counterbalanced gooseneck spout pours in a slow, steady spiral, while a silent induction base holds your water within half a degree of the target temperature for up to an hour. Whether you're brewing a delicate light-roast Ethiopian or a rich French press blend, Lumina takes the guesswork out of the most important variable in your cup.

Why home brewers love it

  • Variable temperature control from 40 °C to 100 °C in one-degree steps
  • Hold mode keeps water at temperature for 60 minutes
  • 0.9 L matte stainless body with a heat-resistant walnut handle
  • Built-in brew timer on the base display — no phone app required

Our story

Lumina began in a two-person workshop in Portland, where a barista and an electrical engineer spent eighteen months prototyping kettles for friends in the local coffee scene. Every unit is still assembled by hand and tested with a full boil-and-pour cycle before it ships, so the kettle that arrives at your door is one we've already brewed with ourselves.
