LumaBrew One

Café-quality pour-over, without the 6 a.m. learning curve.

LumaBrew One is a countertop pour-over coffee maker that heats water to the exact temperature your beans need and pulses it over the grounds in a slow, spiral pattern — the same motion a skilled barista makes by hand. The result is a clean, sweet cup every single morning, whether you're half-awake or hosting brunch.

Features

A precision thermostat holds temperature within half a degree between 195°F and 205°F, while a built-in scale weighs your grounds and water in real time to keep the brew ratio consistent. Three saved profiles let you switch between a light Ethiopian roast and a dark Sumatran blend with one tap.

How It Works

Fill the reservoir, add a paper filter and your grounds, then press the single dial on the front. LumaBrew blooms the coffee for 30 seconds, then completes the pour in about three minutes. Cleanup is a rinse of the carafe and a toss of the filter.
