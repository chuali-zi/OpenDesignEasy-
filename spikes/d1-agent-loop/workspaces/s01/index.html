Nimbus Kettle

Precision pours, every single morning.

The Nimbus Kettle is a countertop pour-over kettle built for people who take their coffee seriously but don't want to babysit a stovetop. Its counterbalanced gooseneck spout gives you a slow, steady stream of water, while the hold-warm element keeps your water at the exact temperature you set — anywhere from 140°F to boiling — for up to an hour.

Features

A one-degree temperature dial, a built-in brew timer on the handle, and a matte stainless steel body that stays cool to the touch. The Nimbus boils a full liter in under four minutes and remembers your last three temperature presets, so your Tuesday oolong and your Saturday French press each get the heat they deserve.

Our Story

Nimbus began in a Portland garage in 2022, when two baristas grew tired of kettles that either dribbled or gushed. After forty-one prototypes and countless cups of test coffee, they landed on a spout geometry that pours like a gentle rain — which is exactly where the name comes from.
