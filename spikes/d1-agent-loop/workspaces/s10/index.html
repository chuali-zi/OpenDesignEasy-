NimbusBrew

Precision pouring, perfected at home.

The NimbusBrew smart kettle holds your water at the exact temperature your beans deserve — within half a degree — so every pour-over tastes the way the roaster intended. A counterbalanced gooseneck spout gives you total control over flow rate, while the companion app remembers your favorite brew recipes and walks you through bloom, pour, and drawdown timing step by step. Built from brushed stainless steel with a walnut handle, it looks as good on your counter as it performs in your cup.

Features include a 0.9 L capacity, a 60-minute hold mode, whisper-quiet heating, and an auto shut-off for peace of mind. Whether you're dialing in a delicate Ethiopian or a bold Sumatran, NimbusBrew takes the guesswork out of great coffee.
