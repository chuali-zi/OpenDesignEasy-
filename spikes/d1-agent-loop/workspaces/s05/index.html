Meet DriftMug: the travel mug that stirs itself.

DriftMug is a 350 ml insulated travel mug with a quiet magnetic impeller built into its base. Drop in your cocoa powder, protein mix, or matcha, press the recessed button on the handle, and a gentle vortex blends your drink in about twenty seconds — no spoon, no clumps, no drips down the side of your bag.

We designed DriftMug after one too many chalky shakes on the morning train. The double-walled stainless steel body keeps drinks hot for six hours or cold for twelve, and the whole stirring unit twists out so the mug can go straight into the dishwasher.

Why you'll love it

A single USB-C charge powers roughly sixty stirring cycles, the lid seals with a one-handed flip lock, and a soft-grip silicone sleeve comes in four earthy colors. DriftMug fits standard car cup holders and most bike bottle cages, so your perfectly mixed drink goes wherever you do.
