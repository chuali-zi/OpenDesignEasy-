Your coffee, exactly 62°C. For six straight hours.

The self-heating travel mug that finally ends the era of lukewarm disappointment.

NimbusBrew is a 400 ml insulated travel mug with a hidden trick: a whisper-thin heating element wrapped around the inner steel wall, powered by a removable battery puck in the base. Set your preferred temperature once on the dial at the bottom of the mug, and NimbusBrew holds it there — through your commute, your meetings, and that long walk you keep promising yourself. No app, no Bluetooth, no subscription. Just hot coffee that stays hot.

Why people love it

  • Precision hold: keeps any drink between 50°C and 75°C, accurate to one degree.
  • Six-hour battery: the puck charges over USB-C in 45 minutes and snaps off for washing.
  • Dishwasher-safe body: every part except the battery puck goes straight in the rack.
  • Spill-proof lid: a two-stage seal survives being tossed sideways into a bag.

We built NimbusBrew after one too many thermoses of coffee gone cold by 10 a.m. Three years and forty-one prototypes later, the first production run ships this autumn in Charcoal, Oat, and Ember Red.
